# Official Data Sources & Directory

This repository curates publicly accessible, verified open data regarding Bangladesh. All statistics and datasets are collected directly from primary statutory authorities, ministries, directorates, and international organizations.

---

## 1. Primary Government Ministries & Directorates

### Demographics, Economy & Labor
- **[Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS)](https://bbs.gov.bd/)**
  - Population and Housing Census (2022, 2011)
  - Labor Force Survey (LFS 2022, 2024 quarterly bulletins)
  - Statistical Yearbook of Bangladesh
  - Report on Agriculture and Rural Statistics
  - Consumer Price Index (CPI) and National Accounts (GDP)
- **[Bangladesh Bank (Central Bank of Bangladesh)](https://www.bb.org.bd/)**
  - Monthly Economic Trends and Balance of Payments
  - Scheduled Bank Statistics & Branch Networks
  - Wage Earners' Remittance Inflows & Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)
- **[Ministry of Labour and Employment (MoLE)](https://mole.gov.bd/) & [Minimum Wage Board](http://www.mwb.gov.bd/)**
  - Gazetted Minimum Wage Schedules across Ready Made Garments (RMG), Tannery, Ship-breaking, Pharmaceuticals, and Tea Gardens
- **[Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training (BMET)](http://www.bmet.gov.bd/)**
  - Overseas Migrant Workers Deployment Statistics by Country of Destination and Skill Category

### Agriculture, Fisheries & Livestock
- **[Department of Fisheries (DoF)](https://fisheries.gov.bd/)**
  - Yearbook of Fisheries Statistics of Bangladesh
  - Inland (Capture & Culture) and Marine Fisheries Production
  - Fish Hatchery, Nursery, and Prawn/Shrimp (Bagda/Golda) Statistics
- **[Department of Livestock Services (DLS)](https://dls.gov.bd/)**
  - Annual Livestock and Poultry Census Reports
  - Production of Milk, Meat, and Eggs vs National Nutritional Demand Targets
- **[Department of Agricultural Extension (DAE)](https://dae.gov.bd/)**
  - Crop Zoning, Agro-Ecological Zones (AEZ), and Seasonal Crop Calendars

### Infrastructure, Transport & Communication
- **[Roads and Highways Department (RHD)](https://rhd.gov.bd/)**
  - National Highway (N-Series), Regional Highway (R-Series), and Zilla Road (Z-Series) Database
- **[Bangladesh Bridge Authority (BBA)](https://bba.gov.bd/)**
  - Technical Specifications of Padma Multipurpose Bridge, Bangabandhu Jamuna Bridge, and Karnaphuli Tunnel
- **[Chittagong Port Authority (CPA)](https://cpa.gov.bd/) & [Mongla Port Authority (MPA)](https://mpa.gov.bd/)**
  - Seaport Terminal Throughput, Container Volumes (TEUs), and Cargo Capacity
- **[Bangladesh Inland Water Transport Authority (BIWTA)](https://biwta.gov.bd/)**
  - River Ports, Passenger Terminals, Launch Routes, and Waterways Navigability
- **[Bangladesh Land Port Authority (BLPA)](https://bsbk.gov.bd/)**
  - International Border Land Ports and ICD Facilities
- **[Bangladesh Railway (BR)](https://railway.gov.bd/)**
  - Operational Zone Mileage, Station Directories, Gauge Classification, and Timetables

### Energy, Power & Natural Resources
- **[Bangladesh Power Development Board (BPDB)](https://bpdb.gov.bd/)**
  - Power Generation Plants Directory, Capacity (MW), Fuel Mix, and Generation Trends
- **[Power Grid Bangladesh (PGCB)](https://pgcb.gov.bd/)**
  - High-Voltage National Transmission Network, Grid Substations, and Cross-Border Interconnections
- **[Power Division / Distribution Utilities](https://powerdivision.gov.bd/)**
  - Performance and Consumer Counts for BREB, DPDC, DESCO, NESCO, WZPDCL
- **[Petrobangla (Bangladesh Oil, Gas and Mineral Corporation)](https://petrobangla.org.bd/)**
  - Field-wise Daily Natural Gas Production (MMCFD) and Hydrocarbon Reserves
- **[Sustainable and Renewable Energy Development Authority (SREDA)](https://sreda.gov.bd/)**
  - National Database of Renewable Energy, Utility Solar Parks, and Rooftop Net-Metering

### Environment, Rivers & Natural Disasters
- **[Bangladesh Water Development Board (BWDB)](https://bwdb.gov.bd/) & [FFWC](http://www.ffwc.gov.bd/)**
  - Transboundary Rivers Database, Danger Levels, and River Water Level Monitoring Stations
- **[Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD)](https://bmd.gov.bd/)**
  - Historical Tropical Cyclones Records, Wind Speeds, Storm Surges, and Agromet Normals
- **[Ministry of Disaster Management and Relief (MoDMR)](https://modmr.gov.bd/) & [DDM](http://www.ddm.gov.bd/)**
  - Multi-Purpose Cyclone Shelters and Emergency Killa Capacities
- **[Bangladesh Forest Department (BFD)](https://bforest.gov.bd/)**
  - National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Forest Land Types, and Mangrove Afforestation
- **[Department of Environment (DoE)](https://doe.gov.bd/)**
  - Daily Air Quality Index (AQI), Ecologically Critical Areas (ECAs), and Water Quality

### Trade & Commerce
- **[Export Promotion Bureau (EPB)](https://epb.gov.bd/)**
  - Harmonized System (HS) Commodity Export Volumes and Country-wise Market Destinations
- **[Bangladesh Export Processing Zones Authority (BEPZA)](https://bepza.gov.bd/)**
  - Operational Profile of 8 National EPZs, Foreign Enterprises, Investment, and Employment

### Housing & Urban Development
- **[Local Government Division (LGD)](https://lgd.gov.bd/)**
  - 12 City Corporations, Pourashavas (Municipalities), and Union Parishad Administrative Data

## 2. Research & Academic AI Repositories
- **[Hugging Face Hub](https://huggingface.co/)** — Pre-trained Bangla models, corpora, and evaluations
- **[AI4Bharat (IIT Madras)](https://ai4bharat.org/)** — IndicTrans2 Bengali-English translation corpora
- **[Bhasha-Protichhobi / CSE BUET NLP](https://github.com/csebuetnlp)** — BanglaBERT, BanglaRQA, BanglaParaphrase
- **[Mozilla Common Voice](https://commonvoice.mozilla.org/)** — Crowdsourced native Bengali speech corpus
- **[Bengali.AI](https://bengali.ai/)** — Spontaneous Bengali speech and OCR benchmark datasets
- **[IUCN Bangladesh](https://www.iucn.org/bangladesh)** — Red List of Threatened Animals of Bangladesh
